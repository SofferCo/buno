Images 1 and 2 are example sheets from one logo designer. In every row: left, a photo; right, the black logo mark he drew from that photo. Study exactly how he abstracts an animal: a few long perfectly straight edges; a handful of perfect circular arcs (rump, belly, tail, head); flat planes instead of soft curves; the back often one straight line; the tail one bold simple shape; legs as simple straight-sided shapes, the far legs detached from the body or separated from it by a thin white gap; one small white eye or none; no fur, no small bumps, no wobble. Image 3 is a new animal, cut out of its photo. Draw the logo mark of this exact animal the way this designer would. Keep its exact pose and proportions and keep the mark at the same position and size in the frame as the animal in image 3 (same head, back, tail and feet positions), but redraw the outline with his bold geometric simplification instead of tracing it. Output: one solid black mark on a pure white square background. Pure black and pure white only, crisp edges, no grey, no outlines, no guide lines, no text, no photo.
